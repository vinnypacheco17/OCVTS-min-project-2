About the Cells Games

The "Cell Games" can refer either to the famous martial arts tournament in Dragon Ball Z hosted by the villain Perfect Cell, or to educational biology video games designed to teach cell structure and organelle functions.

Information for the Cell Games

The Setup: After achieving his "Perfect" form, Cell broadcasts a global message announcing a winner-take-all tournament at the custom-built Cell Games Arena. The Stakes: If Cell wins, he will kill everyone on Earth. If he loses, he leaves the planet in peace. The Rules: Traditional one-on-one matches with no ring-outs allowed once Cell decides to scrap the safety rules

Anime Episodes: Dragon Ball Z episodes 166 through 194 covering The Cell Game
